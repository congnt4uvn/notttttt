# DAY 259 — Hedging

B2 cần học cách **không khẳng định quá tuyệt đối**.

Thay:

> Technology causes stress.

Có thể nói:

> Technology **can contribute to** stress.

Hoặc:

> Technology **may contribute to** stress in some cases.

Học:

* may
* might
* could
* tends to
* appears to
* seems to
* is likely to
* to some extent
