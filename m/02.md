
# English Speaking Practice — Day: Describe My House 🏠

Level: A1–A2 | Practice time: 5 minutes | Topic: My House

Practice speaking aloud. Read slowly the first time, then repeat without looking at the Vietnamese translation.

## 3. Five-minute speaking practice 🎤

## Minute 1 — Introduce your house

Read these sentences aloud.

Đọc to các câu sau.

1\. I live in a small house.

Tôi sống trong một ngôi nhà nhỏ.

2\. My house is in a quiet neighborhood.

Nhà tôi nằm trong một khu dân cư yên tĩnh.

3\. My house is not very big, but it is comfortable.

Nhà tôi không quá lớn nhưng rất thoải mái.

4\. I live with my family.

Tôi sống cùng gia đình.

## Minute 2 — Describe the rooms

5\. My house has three bedrooms, a kitchen, and a living room.

Nhà tôi có ba phòng ngủ, một nhà bếp và một phòng khách.

6\. The living room is spacious and bright.

Phòng khách rộng rãi và sáng sủa.

7\. There is a sofa and a television in the living room.

Có một chiếc ghế sofa và một chiếc tivi trong phòng khách.

8\. The kitchen is clean and modern.

Nhà bếp sạch sẽ và hiện đại.

9\. My bedroom is small, but I like it very much.

Phòng ngủ của tôi nhỏ nhưng tôi rất thích nó.

## Minute 3 — Talk about your favorite room

10\. My favorite room is my bedroom.

Phòng yêu thích của tôi là phòng ngủ.

11\. I usually relax and listen to music in my bedroom.

Tôi thường thư giãn và nghe nhạc trong phòng ngủ.

12\. There is a bed, a desk, and a wardrobe in my room.

Có một chiếc giường, một cái bàn và một tủ quần áo trong phòng tôi.

13\. I sometimes read books or use my computer there.

Thỉnh thoảng tôi đọc sách hoặc sử dụng máy tính ở đó.

14\. I feel happy and relaxed when I am at home.

Tôi cảm thấy vui vẻ và thư giãn khi ở nhà.

## Minute 4 — Describe your daily life at home

15\. I usually wake up early in the morning.

Tôi thường thức dậy sớm vào buổi sáng.

16\. I have breakfast in the kitchen.

Tôi ăn sáng trong nhà bếp.

17\. In the evening, I watch television or spend time with my family.

Vào buổi tối, tôi xem tivi hoặc dành thời gian cho gia đình.

18\. At weekends, I clean my house and organize my room.

Vào cuối tuần, tôi dọn nhà và sắp xếp phòng của mình.

19\. My home is a great place to rest after work.

Nhà là nơi tuyệt vời để nghỉ ngơi sau giờ làm việc.

## Minute 5 — Give your opinion

20\. I really love my house.

Tôi thực sự yêu ngôi nhà của mình.

21\. It is not perfect, but it is special to me.

Nó không hoàn hảo nhưng rất đặc biệt đối với tôi.

22\. I want to make my house more beautiful in the future.

Tôi muốn làm cho ngôi nhà đẹp hơn trong tương lai.

23\. For me, home is the best place in the world.

Đối với tôi, nhà là nơi tuyệt vời nhất trên thế giới.

## 4. Full speaking paragraph — Bài nói hoàn chỉnh

Practice this paragraph several times. Aim to speak naturally for about 1–2 minutes, then extend it by adding details about your real home.

My House

I live in a comfortable house with my family. My house is in a quiet neighborhood. It is not very big, but it is clean and beautiful.

My house has three bedrooms, a kitchen, a bathroom, and a living room. The living room is spacious and bright. There is a sofa and a television in the living room. My kitchen is clean, and I usually have breakfast there.

My favorite room is my bedroom because it is quiet and comfortable. There is a bed, a desk, and a wardrobe in my room. I usually relax, listen to music, or use my computer there.

In the evening, I spend time with my family. At weekends, I clean my house and organize my room. I really love my house because I feel safe and happy there. For me, home is the best place to relax after a busy day.

## 5. Practice questions — Câu hỏi luyện nói

Try to answer each question in 2–3 sentences.

| Question                                   | Sample answer                                              |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Where do you live?                      | I live in Ho Chi Minh City. I live in a comfortable house. |
| 2. How many rooms are there in your house? | There are five rooms in my house.                          |
| 3. What is your favorite room?             | My favorite room is my bedroom because it is quiet.        |
| 4. What is in your living room?            | There is a sofa, a television, and a table.                |
| 5. What do you usually do at home?         | I usually watch television and spend time with my family.  |
| 6. What do you like about your house?      | I like my house because it is comfortable and peaceful.    |

## 6. Speaking tips

- First round: Read every sentence aloud.
- Second round: Read the English only, without looking at Vietnamese.
- Third round: Talk about your own house. Change the number of rooms, furniture, and activities to match your real life.
- Final challenge: Speak for five minutes. If you finish early, describe the colors, location, size, furniture, and what you want to change about your house.

Remember: You do not need perfect grammar to start speaking. Focus on clear pronunciation, complete sentences, and confidence.
