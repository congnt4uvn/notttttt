# English Speaking Practice — Daily Routine

Level A1–A2

5 minutes

Everyday English

Goal: Practice speaking English for 5 minutes about your daily routine, from morning to night. Use simple, natural sentences.

## Part 1 — Morning Routine (1 minute)

1. I usually wake up at seven o'clock in the morning.
2. I get out of bed and brush my teeth.
3. Then, I wash my face and take a shower.
4. After that, I get dressed and prepare for work.
5. I usually have bread and eggs for breakfast.
6. I also drink a cup of coffee every morning.
7. While having breakfast, I sometimes check my phone.
8. I leave my house at around eight o'clock.
9. I usually go to work by motorbike.
10. It takes me about twenty minutes to get to work.

## Part 2 — Working Routine (1 minute)

1. I usually start working at eight thirty.
2. First, I turn on my computer and check my emails.
3. Then, I make a list of things I need to do.
4. I work as a software developer.
5. My job is to write code and develop applications.
6. Sometimes, I have meetings with my teammates.
7. I usually work on different tasks during the day.
8. When I have a problem, I try to find a solution.
9. I enjoy my job because I can learn new things.
10. I always try to finish my work on time.

## Part 3 — Lunch and Afternoon (1 minute)

1. I usually have lunch at twelve o'clock.
2. I often eat rice, meat, and vegetables.
3. Sometimes, I have lunch with my coworkers.
4. We usually talk about work and everyday life.
5. After lunch, I take a short break.
6. Sometimes, I go for a short walk.
7. I go back to work at one o'clock.
8. In the afternoon, I continue working on my tasks.
9. I sometimes feel tired, so I drink some water.
10. I usually finish work at five thirty in the afternoon.

## Part 4 — Evening Routine (1 minute)

1. After work, I go home and relax for a while.
2. Sometimes, I stop at a supermarket to buy food.
3. I usually have dinner at around seven o'clock.
4. After dinner, I spend some time with my family.
5. Sometimes, I watch YouTube videos or listen to music.
6. I also enjoy learning English in my free time.
7. I practice speaking English for thirty minutes every day.
8. I try to learn new vocabulary and useful sentences.
9. Sometimes, I study programming or read a book.
10. I think learning something new every day is important.

## Part 5 — Night Routine (1 minute)

1. At around nine o'clock, I start getting ready for bed.
2. First, I brush my teeth and wash my face.
3. Then, I prepare my clothes for the next day.
4. I sometimes check my phone before going to sleep.
5. I also think about what I need to do tomorrow.
6. I try not to stay up too late.
7. I usually go to bed at around eleven o'clock.
8. I need enough sleep because I want to stay healthy.
9. My daily routine is quite simple, but I enjoy it.
10. That's my typical day. Thank you for listening!

## How to Practice Every Day

- First round: Read all sentences slowly and clearly.
- Second round: Read again without translating into Vietnamese.
- Third round: Change the sentences to match your real daily routine.
- Final round: Speak for 5 minutes without looking at the text.

Speaking tip: Use first, then, after that, sometimes, usually, and finally to connect your sentences naturally. Focus on speaking smoothly, not perfectly.
