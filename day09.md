## 3. Grammar & sentence patterns

### 3.1. Describe responsibilities with action verbs

Đừng chỉ nói "I have many responsibilities". Hãy dùng động từ cụ thể để
người nghe hiểu bạn làm gì.

  Động từ      Ví dụ
  ------------ -----------------------------------------
  manage       I manage daily operations.
  prepare      I prepare weekly reports.
  organize     I organize team meetings.
  support      I support customers.
  coordinate   I coordinate tasks between departments.
  review       I review documents before sending them.
  maintain     I maintain the company website.
  monitor      I monitor project progress.

**Mẫu câu:** - My job involves + V-ing. - My main duties include + V-ing
/ noun. - I'm in charge of + noun / V-ing. - I'm responsible for + noun
/ V-ing.

Ví dụ: - My job involves communicating with customers. - My main duties
include preparing reports and organizing meetings. - I'm in charge of
managing the project schedule.

### 3.2. Present Perfect --- Nói về kinh nghiệm

Dùng **have/has + past participle (V3)** để nói về trải nghiệm hoặc
thành quả có liên quan đến hiện tại, không nhất thiết nêu thời điểm cụ
thể.

-   I have worked in customer service.
-   I have managed several projects.
-   She has developed strong communication skills.
-   We have completed three major projects.

**Câu hỏi thường gặp:** - Have you ever worked with international
clients? - Have you managed a team before? - What skills have you
developed?

**Trả lời:** - Yes, I have. / No, I haven't. - I've worked with
international clients before. - I haven't managed a team yet.

Phân biệt: - **I worked on a project last year.** --- Có thời điểm quá
khứ cụ thể. - **I have worked on several projects.** --- Nói về kinh
nghiệm đến hiện tại.

### 3.3. Explain results with because, so and although

-   **because + lý do:** I stayed late because the deadline was close.
-   **so + kết quả:** The system was slow, so we checked the server.
-   **although + sự tương phản:** Although the task was difficult, we
    finished it on time.

Ví dụ: \> The project was challenging because we had a short deadline.
We divided the work into smaller tasks, so we finished on time.

### 3.4. Talk about strengths and areas to improve

-   One of my strengths is communication.
-   I'm good at organizing tasks.
-   I'm confident when speaking with customers.
-   I need to improve my presentation skills.
-   I'm working on becoming more organized.

Khi phỏng vấn, nói về điểm yếu theo hướng tích cực và trung thực: \> I
sometimes spend too much time checking details, so I'm learning to
manage my time better.

## 4. Useful sentence patterns --- Mẫu câu nâng cấp

### Mô tả công việc

1.  My role mainly involves **\[activity\]**.
2.  A typical day includes **\[activity 1\]** and **\[activity 2\]**.
3.  I'm in charge of **\[responsibility\]**.
4.  I work closely with **\[person/team/department\]**.
5.  I report directly to **\[position/person\]**.

### Mô tả kỹ năng và kinh nghiệm

1.  I have experience in **\[field/activity\]**.
2.  I've worked with **\[tool/customer/team\]**.
3.  One of my strongest skills is **\[skill\]**.
4.  I'm comfortable with **\[task/situation\]**.
5.  I'm still learning how to **\[skill/activity\]**.

### Giải thích vấn đề

1.  We ran into a problem with **\[issue\]**.
2.  The main challenge was **\[problem\]**.
3.  To solve it, we **\[action\]**.
4.  As a result, **\[result\]**.
5.  Next time, I would **\[improvement\]**.

### Cập nhật tiến độ

1.  We've completed **\[task\]**.
2.  We're currently working on **\[task\]**.
3.  We still need to **\[action\]**.
4.  We expect to finish by **\[date/time\]**.
5.  There's a small issue with **\[item\]**.

## 5. Conversation practice --- Hội thoại

### Conversation 1: Giving a work update

**A:** Can you give me a quick update on the project?\
**B:** Sure. We've completed the first part, and we're currently working
on the second part.\
**A:** Are we still on schedule?\
**B:** Mostly, yes. However, we've run into a small technical issue.\
**A:** Does that affect the deadline?\
**B:** Not at the moment. We expect to finish by Thursday.\
**A:** Great. Please let me know if anything changes.\
**B:** Of course. I'll keep you updated.

**Cụm từ:** - give me a quick update --- cập nhật nhanh cho tôi - be on
schedule --- đúng tiến độ - run into an issue --- gặp phải vấn đề - keep
you updated --- tiếp tục cập nhật cho bạn

### Conversation 2: Explaining a problem

**A:** Is everything going well with the new system?\
**B:** Not exactly. We found an issue during testing.\
**A:** What seems to be the problem?\
**B:** Some users can't log in. We're checking the cause now.\
**A:** Do you have a solution?\
**B:** We have a possible solution, but we need to test it first.\
**A:** Okay. Please share the results after testing.\
**B:** Sure. I'll send you an update this afternoon.

### Conversation 3: Talking about strengths in an interview

**Interviewer:** What are your main strengths?\
**Candidate:** I'm organized, responsible, and good at solving
problems.\
**Interviewer:** Can you give me an example?\
**Candidate:** In my previous role, I helped my team organize tasks and
meet deadlines.\
**Interviewer:** What would you like to improve?\
**Candidate:** I'd like to improve my presentation skills and become
more confident when speaking English.

## 6. Speaking practice --- Luyện nói

### Topic 1: What are your main responsibilities? (1--2 minutes)

Trả lời: 1. What is your job title? 2. What are your main duties? 3.
What do you do every day? 4. Who do you work with? 5. What tools or
skills do you use? 6. What is the most important part of your role?

**Mẫu:** \> My role involves managing daily tasks and communicating with
customers. I prepare reports, answer questions, and coordinate with
other team members. I also help solve problems when they come up. I need
to be organized and communicate clearly. The most important part of my
job is making sure customers receive good support.

### Topic 2: Talk about your skills (1 minute)

Nói về: - Two strengths. - One skill you are improving. - An example of
when you used a skill.

Mẫu: \> One of my strengths is problem-solving. I'm also good at
organizing my work. However, I'd like to improve my communication
skills, especially when speaking English. I practise by having short
conversations every day.

### Topic 3: Describe a project you worked on (1--2 minutes)

Dùng trình tự: 1. **Background:** What was the project? 2. **Your
role:** What were you responsible for? 3. **Challenge:** What was
difficult? 4. **Action:** What did you do? 5. **Result:** What happened?

Mẫu: \> I worked on a project to improve our customer service process. I
was responsible for collecting feedback and organizing the information.
The main challenge was that we had a short deadline. I discussed the
priorities with my team and divided the work into smaller tasks. As a
result, we completed the project on time and improved the process.

### Topic 4: Describe a problem you solved

Gợi ý: - What happened? - Why was it a problem? - What steps did you
take? - What was the result? - What did you learn?

Mẫu mở đầu: \> Recently, I ran into a problem at work. The main issue
was that...

### Topic 5: Your professional development

Gợi ý: - What skills do you want to develop? - Do you take courses? -
How do you learn? - What kind of role would you like in the future?

Mẫu: \> I want to develop both my technical and communication skills. I
usually learn through online courses and practical projects. In the
future, I hope to take on more responsibility and work on more
challenging projects.

## 7. Role-play --- Tình huống thực tế

### Situation A: Your manager asks for a progress update

Bạn cần trình bày: - Việc đã hoàn thành. - Việc đang thực hiện. - Vấn đề

Cố gắng dùng: - We've completed... - We're currently working on... -
We've run into... - We expect to finish by...

Mẫu mở đầu: \> Hi, do you have a minute? I'm having a little trouble
with...

Tiếp tục giải thích vấn đề, nhờ hỗ trợ và thống nhất bước tiếp theo.

Trả lời thành tiếng: 1. What are your main responsibilities? 2. What
experience do you have? 3. What are your greatest strengths? 4. Tell me
about a problem you solved. 5. What skills would you like to improve?

## 8. Exercises --- Bài tập

### Exercise 1: Complete the collocations

Điền từ phù hợp: **progress, a decision, a deadline, an issue,

2.  The team made \_\_\_\_\_\_ after discussing the options.
3.  We need to meet \_\_\_\_\_\_ on Friday.
4.  We found \_\_\_\_\_\_ during testing.
5.  Let's review our \_\_\_\_\_\_ at the end of the week.

### Exercise 2: Present Perfect

Chia động từ trong ngoặc.

1.  I \_\_\_\_\_\_ (work) with international customers before.
2.  She \_\_\_\_\_\_ (manage) several projects.
3.  We \_\_\_\_\_\_ (not finish) the report yet.
4.  \_\_\_\_\_\_ you ever \_\_\_\_\_\_ (lead) a team?

### Exercise 3: Choose the correct word

1.  My job involves (organize / organizing) meetings.
2.  I'm in charge of (manage / managing) the schedule.
3.  We (have completed / completed) the task yesterday.
4.  I (have worked / worked) here since 2023.
5.  The team is currently (check / checking) the issue.

### Exercise 4: Connect the ideas

Dùng **because, so, although**.

1.  The deadline was close. We worked extra hours.
2.  The task was difficult. We completed it on time.
3.  The system was slow. We contacted the technical team.

## 11. Daily speaking challenge

**Ghi âm 2 phút:** "My Responsibilities, Skills and a Challenge I
Solved".

Yêu cầu: - Giới thiệu vai trò hoặc công việc mong muốn. - Nêu ít nhất 3
trách nhiệm. - Nói về 2 kỹ năng. - Mô tả một vấn đề và cách giải
quyết. - Nêu kết quả hoặc điều bạn học được.

### Checklist

-   [ ] Tôi mô tả được ít nhất 3 nhiệm vụ cụ thể.
-   [ ] Tôi dùng được "My job involves..." và "I'm in charge of...".
-   [ ] Tôi nói được về kinh nghiệm bằng Present Perfect.
-   [ ] Tôi giải thích được vấn đề bằng because, so hoặc although.
-   [ ] Tôi có thể trình bày một dự án trong khoảng 1--2 phút.

**Mẹo luyện nói:** Ghi âm lần đầu không nhìn bài. Nghe lại và ghi ra 3
điểm cần cải thiện. Sau đó nói lần hai, cố gắng diễn đạt tự nhiên hơn
thay vì đọc thuộc lòng.

## 12. Preview --- Day 10

**DAY 10 --- OFFICE COMMUNICATION**

Ngày tiếp theo: - Chào hỏi và trò chuyện với đồng nghiệp. - Hỏi và trả
lời trong cuộc họp. - Nhờ giúp đỡ, đưa ra đề xuất và phản hồi lịch sự. -
Viết và nói những câu ngắn trong môi trường công sở. - Thực hành hội
thoại với đồng nghiệp và quản lý.
