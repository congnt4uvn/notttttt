## 6. 🎤 Luyện nói liên tục 7 phút

Chia bài nói thành 7 phần, mỗi phần khoảng 1 phút. Đây là các ý gợi nhớ, không phải kịch bản cần học thuộc.

Minute 1 — Introduction

Giới thiệu

- What is online learning?
- Do you learn English online?
- Why is this topic important to you?

Câu gợi ý: Nowadays, many people choose to learn English online because it is convenient.

Minute 2 — Flexibility

Ưu điểm 1

- Study anytime and anywhere.
- Learn before or after work.
- Manage your own schedule.

Câu gợi ý: One major advantage is that I can study whenever I have free time.

Minute 3 — Cost and resources

Ưu điểm 2

- Save money and transportation costs.
- Access videos, apps and online courses.
- Repeat lessons whenever needed.

Câu gợi ý: Another benefit is that online learning can be more affordable than traditional classes.

Minute 4 — Distractions

Nhược điểm 1

- Social media and mobile phones.
- Noise at home.
- Difficulty concentrating.

Câu gợi ý: However, studying at home can sometimes make it difficult to concentrate.

Minute 5 — Interaction

Nhược điểm 2

- Less face-to-face communication.
- Fewer opportunities for spontaneous conversations.
- Internet and technical issues.

Câu gợi ý: Another disadvantage is the lack of face-to-face interaction.

Minute 6 — Personal experience

Trải nghiệm

- Describe your learning routine.
- Explain a challenge you have faced.
- Share how you improve your speaking skills.

Câu gợi ý: In my experience, studying online helps me save time, but I need to be more disciplined.

Minute 7 — Conclusion

Kết luận

- Summarize the advantages and disadvantages.
- Explain what learning method suits your needs.
- End with a final thought.

Câu gợi ý: In conclusion, online learning has both advantages and disadvantages, and success depends on how we use it.

## 8. 💬 Bài nói tham khảo

Dùng để hiểu cách triển khai ý. Không học thuộc hoặc đọc nguyên văn khi thực hiện thử thách 7 phút.

# The Advantages and Disadvantages of Learning English Online

Nowadays, learning English online has become very popular. Many people choose this method because it is convenient and flexible. I also think online learning is a useful way to improve English skills.

One major advantage of learning English online is flexibility. Students can study anytime and anywhere. For example, I can study English after work or during my free time. I don't need to travel to a language center, so I can save a lot of time.

Another advantage is that online learning can be affordable. There are many free videos, websites, and applications that help students learn English. We can also watch recorded lessons again if we don't understand something.

However, online learning also has some disadvantages. The first problem is distraction. When I study at home, I sometimes check my phone or use social media. As a result, I may lose concentration.

Another disadvantage is the lack of face-to-face interaction. Learning a language requires communication and speaking practice. When students study alone, they may have fewer opportunities to communicate with teachers and other students.

In my experience, online learning is convenient, but it requires self-discipline and motivation. I think students should create a daily study schedule and practice speaking regularly.

In conclusion, learning English online has both advantages and disadvantages. It offers flexibility, convenience, and affordable resources, but it can also cause distractions and reduce interaction. I believe combining online learning with real-life communication can help students develop their English skills.

## 9. 🧠 Cách luyện nói không dịch từng câu

Suy nghĩ bằng từ khóa

Thay vì dịch cả câu tiếng Việt, hãy nhớ các từ khóa tiếng Anh: flexibility → save time → after work → convenient.

Nói câu ngắn trước

Ví dụ: "I study English online. It is convenient. I can study after work." Sau đó nối ý bằng because, so, however.

Lặp lại bằng cách diễn đạt khác

Ví dụ: "Online learning is flexible." → "I can choose my own study time."

Tự ghi âm và nghe lại

Chú ý những chỗ dừng quá lâu, câu chưa rõ nghĩa và từ nối bị lặp lại. Luyện lại phần đó bằng cách diễn đạt tự nhiên hơn.
