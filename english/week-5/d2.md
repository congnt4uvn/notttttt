# 2. 🔵 Verb To Be

### Công thức

**I + am**

3. are

5. Are

# 5. 🔵 PRESENT SIMPLE

## Dùng để nói về:

* thói quen
* công việc
* lịch trình
* sự thật

### Công thức

**I / You / We / They + V**

> I work every day.

**He / She / It + V-s/es**

> She works every day.

### Ví dụ thực tế

> **I get up at 7.**
> Tôi thức dậy lúc 7 giờ.

> **I go to work at 8.**
> Tôi đi làm lúc 8 giờ.

> **She works from Monday to Friday.**
> Cô ấy làm việc từ thứ Hai đến thứ Sáu.

# 6. 🔴 DON'T / DOESN'T

### I / You / We / They

**don't + V**

> I don't work on Sunday.

### He / She / It

**doesn't + V**

> She doesn't work on Sunday.

⚠️ Sau **doesn't**, động từ trở lại nguyên mẫu.

❌ She doesn't works.

✅ She doesn't work.

### ✏️ Bài tập

1. I ___ like coffee.
2. She ___ like coffee.
3. They ___ work on Sunday.
4. He ___ play football.

**Đáp án:**

1. don't
2. doesn't
3. don't
4. doesn't

# 7. ❓ DO / DOES

### Câu hỏi

**Do + I/you/we/they + V...?**

> Do you work here?

**Does + he/she/it + V...?**

> Does she work here?

### Hội thoại thực tế

**A:** Do you work here?
**B:** Yes, I do.

**A:** Does your brother live in Ho Chi Minh City?
**B:** No, he doesn't.

### ✏️ Bài tập

1. ___ you speak English?
2. ___ she work here?
3. ___ they like coffee?
4. ___ he drive to work?

**Đáp án:**

1. Do
2. Does
3. Do
4. Does

### Nhiều vật

### Ví dụ đời sống

### ✏️ Bài tập

**Đáp án:**

1. There is
2. There are
3. There is

# 🎤 BÀI NÓI MẪU HOÀN CHỈNH

My name is Cong. I am from Vietnam, and I live in Ho Chi Minh City. I am a developer.

I live with my family. I have a small family. I like watching movies, listening to music, and learning English.

I usually get up at 7 o'clock. I have breakfast at 7:30. Then I go to work at 8 o'clock. I work until 5 o'clock. After work, I usually go home and have dinner.

I like coffee, but I don't like very sweet drinks. On weekends, I usually relax at home or go out with my friends.

Last weekend, I visited my parents. Next weekend, I am going to spend time with my family.

I am learning English because I want to speak English more confidently in my daily life.
