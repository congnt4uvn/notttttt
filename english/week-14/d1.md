DAY 91 — Present Perfect vs Past Simple
Đây là bài rất quan trọng.

Past Simple
Thời điểm cụ thể:

I visited Da Nang last year.

Present Perfect
Kinh nghiệm:

I have visited Da Nang.

Practice
So sánh:

I went to Korea in 2024.
I have been to Korea.
I worked there for two years.
I have worked there for two years.
Speaking
Tell me about places you have visited.
