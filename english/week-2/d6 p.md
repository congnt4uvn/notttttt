# 🇬🇧 DAY 13 — RESTAURANT

Hôm nay chúng ta tập trung vào **tiếng Anh dùng khi đi nhà hàng**. Mục tiêu là sau bài này bạn có thể:

* Gọi món 🍔
* Hỏi menu 📋
* Gọi đồ uống ☕
* Hỏi giá 💰
* Yêu cầu tính tiền 💵
* Nói chuyện cơ bản với waiter
* Thực hành một đoạn hội thoại thực tế

---

### 🧠 Mẹo nhớ nhanh

Hãy tưởng tượng bạn bước vào nhà hàng:

**table → menu → order → food → bill**

> 🪑 Table → 📋 Menu → 🗣️ Order → 🍔 Food → 💵 Bill

Chỉ cần nhớ chuỗi này là bạn đã nhớ được một phần lớn bài.

---

# 2. 🗣️ Useful Sentences — Mẫu câu quan trọng

## A. Xin menu

### **Can I have the menu, please?**

→ Tôi có thể xin menu được không?

Cách nói tự nhiên:

> **Can I have the menu, please?**

Ví dụ:

> A: Can I have the menu, please?
> B: Sure. Here you are.

---

## B. Gọi đồ uống

### **Can I have a coffee, please?**

→ Cho tôi một ly cà phê được không?

Có thể thay **coffee**:

> Can I have a tea, please?
> Can I have some water, please?
> Can I have an orange juice, please?

### 🧠 Công thức

**Can I have + food/drink?**

Ví dụ:

> Can I have a coffee?
> Can I have a sandwich?
> Can I have some water?

---

## C. Gọi món

### **I'd like a hamburger.**

→ Tôi muốn một hamburger.

**I'd like = I would like**

Đây là mẫu câu **rất quan trọng khi gọi món**.

Ví dụ:

> I'd like a hamburger.
> I'd like a chicken sandwich.
> I'd like a bowl of rice.

### 🧠 Công thức

**I'd like + món ăn.**

---

# 3. 💰 Hỏi giá

### **How much is it?**

→ Nó bao nhiêu tiền?

Ví dụ:

> A: How much is it?
> B: It's ten dollars.

Hoặc:

> **How much is this?**
> → Cái này bao nhiêu tiền?

---

# 4. 💵 Yêu cầu hóa đơn

### **Can I have the bill, please?**

→ Cho tôi xin hóa đơn được không?

Đây là câu rất thực tế khi ăn nhà hàng.

Ví dụ:

> A: Can I have the bill, please?
> B: Sure. I'll bring it right away.

---

# 5. ⭐ 5 mẫu câu cần thuộc lòng

Nếu bạn chỉ học 5 câu hôm nay, hãy học:

### ① Can I have the menu, please?

→ Cho tôi xin menu.

### ② Can I have a coffee, please?

→ Cho tôi một ly cà phê.

### ③ I'd like a hamburger.

→ Tôi muốn một hamburger.

### ④ How much is it?

→ Bao nhiêu tiền?

### ⑤ Can I have the bill, please?

→ Cho tôi xin hóa đơn.

# 6. 🧩 Grammar — "Can I have...?"

Đây là cấu trúc cực kỳ hữu ích.

### Công thức:

> **Can I have + noun?**

Ví dụ:

| English                 | Vietnamese                        |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Can I have a coffee?    | Cho tôi một cà phê được không?    |
| Can I have some water?  | Cho tôi một ít nước được không?   |
| Can I have a hamburger? | Cho tôi một hamburger được không? |
| Can I have the menu?    | Cho tôi xin menu được không?      |
| Can I have the bill?    | Cho tôi xin hóa đơn được không?   |

### ❌ Không nên chỉ học:

> Coffee.

### ✅ Hãy tập nói:

> **Can I have a coffee, please?**

Mục tiêu là học **cả cụm câu**, không chỉ học từng từ.

# 12. 🗣️ Role-play — Customer ↔ Waiter

Đây là phần **quan trọng nhất**.

Hãy đọc thành tiếng cả hai vai.

### 🍽️ Conversation

**Waiter:** Hello. Welcome to our restaurant.

**Customer:** Hi.

**Waiter:** How many people?

**Customer:** Two people, please.

**Waiter:** Right this way. Here is your table.

**Customer:** Thank you.

**Waiter:** Can I get you something to drink?

**Customer:** Yes. Can I have a coffee, please?

**Waiter:** You're welcome.

## 1. Ordering Food (Gọi món ăn)

Tình huống: Khách hàng gọi món tại nhà hàng.

**Waiter:** Good evening. Are you ready to order?

**Customer:** Yes, I'm ready.

**Waiter:** What would you like?

**Customer:** I'd like a chicken sandwich, please.

**Waiter:** Would you like some fries?

**Customer:** Yes, please.

**Waiter:** Anything to drink?

**Customer:** A glass of orange juice, please.

**Waiter:** Sure. I'll be right back.

**Customer:** Thank you.

## 2. Asking for the Menu (Yêu cầu thực đơn)

**Waiter:** Hello. Welcome to our restaurant.

**Customer:** Hello. Can I see the menu, please?

**Waiter:** Of course. Here you are.

**Customer:** Thank you. What do you recommend?

**Waiter:** I recommend our grilled chicken.

**Customer:** Is it spicy?

**Waiter:** No, it's not spicy.

**Customer:** Great. I'll have that, please.

**Waiter:** Would you like rice with that?

**Customer:** Yes, please.

## 3. Ordering Drinks (Gọi đồ uống)

**Waiter:** Good afternoon. What would you like to drink?

**Customer:** I'd like a cup of coffee, please.

**Waiter:** Hot or iced coffee?

**Customer:** Iced coffee, please.

**Waiter:** Would you like any sugar?

**Customer:** Yes, just a little.

**Waiter:** Anything else?

**Customer:** Can I also have a bottle of water?

**Waiter:** Sure. No problem.

**Customer:** Thank you very much.

## 4. At a Fast Food Restaurant (Tại nhà hàng thức ăn nhanh)

**Staff:** Hello. Welcome to our restaurant.

**Customer:** Hi. Can I have a cheeseburger, please?

**Staff:** Sure. Would you like a small or large meal?

**Customer:** A large meal, please.

**Staff:** Would you like fries and a drink?

**Customer:** Yes. I'd like fries and a Coke.

**Staff:** Is that for here or to go?

**Customer:** For here, please.

**Staff:** That'll be ten dollars.

**Customer:** Here you go.

**Staff:** Thank you. Please wait a moment.

## 5. Asking About Food (Hỏi về món ăn)

**Customer:** Excuse me. Can I ask you something?

**Waiter:** Of course. How can I help you?

**Customer:** Does this dish contain beef?

**Waiter:** Yes, it does.

**Customer:** Do you have any chicken dishes?

**Waiter:** Yes. We have grilled chicken and fried chicken.

**Customer:** Is the grilled chicken good?

**Waiter:** Yes. It's one of our most popular dishes.

**Customer:** Okay. I'll have the grilled chicken.

**Waiter:** Excellent choice.

## 6. Having Lunch with a Friend (Ăn trưa với bạn)

**Tom:** Hi, Cong. What would you like to eat?

**Cong:** I'm not sure. What about you?

**Tom:** I think I'll have some noodles.

**Cong:** That sounds good. I like noodles, too.

**Tom:** Would you like some chicken?

**Cong:** Yes, please. I love chicken.

**Tom:** What would you like to drink?

**Cong:** I'd like a glass of iced tea.

**Tom:** Okay. Let's order now.

**Cong:** Sure. I'm hungry.

## 7. Making a Reservation (Đặt bàn tại nhà hàng)

**Receptionist:** Good evening. How can I help you?

**Customer:** Hello. I'd like to book a table, please.

**Receptionist:** Certainly. For how many people?

**Customer:** For four people.

**Receptionist:** What time would you like to come?

**Customer:** At 7 p.m. tonight.

**Receptionist:** May I have your name, please?

**Customer:** My name is Cong.

**Receptionist:** Okay. A table for four at 7 p.m.

**Customer:** That's right. Thank you.

**Receptionist:** You're welcome. See you tonight.

## 8. Asking for the Bill (Yêu cầu thanh toán)

**Customer:** Excuse me. Can I have the bill, please?

**Waiter:** Of course. Just a moment.

**Customer:** Thank you.

**Waiter:** Here is your bill. That's twenty-five dollars.

**Customer:** Can I pay by credit card?

**Waiter:** Yes, certainly.

**Customer:** Here is my card.

**Waiter:** Thank you. Please enter your PIN.

**Customer:** Okay. Done.

**Waiter:** Your payment is successful.

**Customer:** Thank you. Have a nice day.

**Waiter:** You too. Goodbye.

## 9. Complaining About Food (Phản hồi về món ăn)

**Customer:** Excuse me. Can you help me, please?

**Waiter:** Yes, of course. What's the problem?

**Customer:** My soup is a little cold.

**Waiter:** I'm very sorry about that.

**Customer:** Could you heat it up, please?

**Waiter:** Certainly. I'll bring you a new bowl.

**Customer:** Thank you. I appreciate it.

**Waiter:** You're welcome. Sorry for the inconvenience.

**Customer:** No problem. Thank you for your help.

## 10. Ordering Takeaway (Gọi đồ ăn mang về)

**Staff:** Hello. How can I help you?

**Customer:** Hi. I'd like to order some food to take away.

**Staff:** Sure. What would you like?

**Customer:** I'd like two hamburgers and one portion of fries.

**Staff:** Would you like anything to drink?

**Customer:** Two bottles of water, please.

**Staff:** Okay. Anything else?

**Customer:** No, that's all.

**Staff:** It will take about fifteen minutes.

**Customer:** That's fine. How much is it?

**Staff:** It's fifteen dollars.

**Customer:** Here you go. Thank you.

**Staff:** Thank you. Have a nice day.
