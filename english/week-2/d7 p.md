# 1. 🔥 QUICK REVIEW — ÔN NHANH

## Present Simple

### Với I / You / We / They

> I work.
> You work.
> We work.
> They work.

### Với He / She / It

> He works.
> She works.
> It works.

⚠️ Nhớ:

> **He / She / It → động từ thường thêm s/es**

Ví dụ:

* I work.
* She **works**.
* I like coffee.
* He **likes** coffee.
* They eat rice.
* She **eats** rice.

---

# 2. ❓ DO / DOES

## Câu hỏi

| Chủ ngữ             | Cấu trúc                   |
| ------------------- | -------------------------- |
| I / you / we / they | **Do + subject + verb?**   |
| he / she / it       | **Does + subject + verb?** |

Ví dụ:

> Do you work?

→ Bạn có làm việc không?

> Do you like coffee?

→ Bạn có thích cà phê không?

> Does he work here?

→ Anh ấy làm việc ở đây à?

> Does she speak English?

→ Cô ấy nói tiếng Anh à?

### ⚠️ Quy tắc cực kỳ quan trọng

Sau **does**, động từ trở về nguyên mẫu.

❌ Does she **likes** coffee?

✅ Does she **like** coffee?

❌ Does he **works** here?

✅ Does he **work** here?

---

# 3. ❌ DON'T / DOESN'T

### I / You / We / They

> don't + verb

Ví dụ:

> I don't like coffee.

> They don't work on Sunday.

### He / She / It

> doesn't + verb

Ví dụ:

> He doesn't like coffee.

> She doesn't work here.

⚠️ Sau **doesn't** cũng dùng động từ nguyên mẫu.

❌ She doesn't **likes** coffee.

✅ She doesn't **like** coffee.

---

# 4. 🧠 BÀI TẬP 1 — DO HAY DOES?

| --------------------------- | -------- |

> **He / She → DOES**
> **I / You / We / They → DO**

# 12. 🗣️ CÂU TIẾNG ANH DÙNG THỰC TẾ Ở NHÀ HÀNG

> **Can I have a coffee?**

> **Can I have the menu, please?**

Cho tôi xem thực đơn được không?

> **I'd like some chicken and rice.**

Tôi muốn một ít gà và cơm.

### Hỏi giá

> **How much is it?**

Bao nhiêu tiền?

### Gọi hóa đơn

> **Can I have the bill, please?**

Cho tôi xin hóa đơn.

### Một đoạn hội thoại ngắn

**Waiter:** What would you like?

**You:** Yes. Can I have a coffee?

**Waiter:** Sure.

**You:** Thank you.
