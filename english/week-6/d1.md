# 🇬🇧 DAY 36 — READING PAST EVENTS

### Đọc hiểu các sự kiện trong quá khứ

**Mục tiêu hôm nay:**
Sau bài này, bạn có thể đọc một đoạn văn ngắn về **hôm qua, cuối tuần, kỳ nghỉ, chuyến du lịch hoặc tuổi thơ**, sau đó trả lời 5 câu hỏi:

> **Who? – Where? – When? – What happened? – Why?**

# 2. ÔN NHANH PAST SIMPLE

Khi đọc chuyện quá khứ, bạn sẽ gặp rất nhiều động từ ở **Past Simple**.

### Động từ thường

> work → worked
> visit → visited
> watch → watched
> play → played
> clean → cleaned

**Ví dụ:**

> I visited my grandmother yesterday.
> Tôi đã thăm bà tôi hôm qua.

> We watched a movie last night.
> Chúng tôi đã xem một bộ phim tối qua.

### Động từ bất quy tắc

> go → went
> eat → ate
> see → saw
> have → had
> take → took
> come → came

**Ví dụ:**

> I went to Da Nang last summer.

> We ate seafood at the beach.

> I saw my old friend yesterday.

# 3. 5 CÂU HỎI QUAN TRỌNG

Khi đọc một câu chuyện, hãy tìm:

### ① WHO? — Ai?

**Who went to Da Nang?**

→ **Tom and his family.**

### ② WHERE? — Ở đâu?

**Where did they go?**

→ **They went to Da Nang.**

### ③ WHEN? — Khi nào?

**When did they go?**

→ **Last summer.**

### ④ WHAT HAPPENED? — Chuyện gì đã xảy ra?

**What happened?**

→ **They visited the beach and ate seafood.**

### ⑤ WHY? — Tại sao?

**Why did they go there?**

→ **Because they wanted to relax.**

# 4. READING 1 — YESTERDAY

### 📖 Đọc đoạn văn

> **Yesterday, Anna woke up at 7 a.m. She had breakfast with her family. Then, she went to work. In the afternoon, she met her friend at a coffee shop. They talked for an hour. Anna went home at 7 p.m. because she was tired.**

### 🇻🇳 Nghĩa

Hôm qua, Anna thức dậy lúc 7 giờ sáng. Cô ấy ăn sáng với gia đình. Sau đó, cô ấy đi làm. Buổi chiều, cô ấy gặp bạn ở một quán cà phê. Họ nói chuyện trong một giờ. Anna về nhà lúc 7 giờ tối vì cô ấy mệt.

---

## ✏️ Bài tập 1

| Câu hỏi                               | Đáp án                                        |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Who?**                              | → **Anna.**                                   |
| **Where did she meet her friend?**    | → **At a coffee shop.**                       |
| **When did she wake up?**             | → **At 7 a.m.**                               |
| **What did she do in the afternoon?** | → **She met her friend and talked with her.** |
| **Why did she go home?**              | → **Because she was tired.**                  |

### 🗣️ Câu nói thực tế

> **What did you do yesterday?**
> Bạn đã làm gì hôm qua?

> **I went to work and met a friend.**

> **What time did you go home?**

> **I went home at 7 p.m.**

# 5. READING 2 — LAST WEEKEND

### 📖 Đọc

> **Last weekend, Mike stayed home on Saturday. He cleaned his room and watched a movie. On Sunday, he went shopping with his sister. They bought some clothes and had lunch at a small restaurant. Mike enjoyed the weekend because he had plenty of free time.**

### ✏️ Bài tập 2

**1. Who stayed home on Saturday?**
→ **Mike.**

**2. When did Mike go shopping?**
→ **On Sunday.**

**3. Who went shopping with Mike?**
→ **His sister.**

**4. What did they buy?**
→ **They bought some clothes.**

**5. Why did Mike enjoy the weekend?**
→ **Because he had plenty of free time.**

### 🗣️ Mẫu câu nói

> **What did you do last weekend?**

→ **I stayed home and watched a movie.**

> **Did you go shopping?**

→ **Yes, I did. I went shopping with my sister.**

# 9. CÔNG THỨC ĐỌC HIỂU SIÊU NHANH

Khi gặp một đoạn văn, **đừng cố dịch từng từ**.

Hãy tìm theo thứ tự:

### 👤 WHO

Ai?

↓

### 📍 WHERE

Ở đâu?

↓

### 🕐 WHEN

Khi nào?

↓

### ⚡ WHAT

Chuyện gì xảy ra?

↓

### ❓ WHY

Tại sao?

Ví dụ:

> **Last Sunday, Tom went to the park with his son because the weather was beautiful. They played football and ate ice cream.**

Bạn chỉ cần lấy:

**WHO:** Tom + his son
**WHERE:** park
**WHEN:** last Sunday
**WHAT:** played football + ate ice cream
**WHY:** beautiful weather

👉 Như vậy bạn đã hiểu **ý chính** mà không cần dịch từng chữ.
