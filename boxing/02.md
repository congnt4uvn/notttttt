Today's mission is to understand and practice the four fundamental punches used in boxing:

1. Jab (1)
2. Cross (2)
3. Hook (3)
4. Uppercut (4)

These four punches form the foundation of almost every boxing combination.

Today's main objective: Learn correct punching mechanics, balance, rotation, and returning your hands to guard.

Do not prioritize punching power yet. Accuracy and technique come first.

## 1. Learning objectives

By the end of today's session, you should be able to:

- Understand the mechanics of four basic punches.
- Coordinate your feet, hips, shoulders, and hands.
- Return to your defensive guard after punching.
- Understand the boxing number system.
- Perform basic punch combinations.
- Practice controlled punches on the heavy bag with your coach.

## 2. Theory — Understanding the four punches

### Punch 1: Jab

[How to Throw the Perfect Jab in Boxing: Step-by-Step Guide](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/AjlaxrOLUCiLSsNffxMR2rfPvQjOd1ajUM7ebAV37OpPUnGa3cD23P_DKSEhPgQLzNGUfS_-wqmmQkkQQVius2Ff12VAzQPfM9cwh2tItpjN5l_IVkF2_TQm90OzifmLxWTLdR36bKlJhWxe_r--M4B35JqkiKmqwPR-aR9RFNY?purpose=inline)

[shadowboxingapp.com](https://shadowboxingapp.com/how-to-jab-boxing/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The jab is a straight punch delivered with your lead hand.

Purpose:

- Measure distance.
- Control your opponent.
- Interrupt attacks.
- Set up combinations.
- Create opportunities for the cross.

Technique:

1. Start in your boxing stance.
2. Keep your rear hand protecting your face.
3. Extend your lead hand straight forward.
4. Rotate your fist appropriately at impact.
5. Keep your shoulder raised to protect your chin.
6. Return your hand immediately to guard.

Common mistakes:

- Dropping the rear hand.
- Reaching too far.
- Leaning forward.
- Leaving the jab extended.

### Punch 2: Cross

[How to Throw a Powerful Cross in Boxing](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/drFgodfxyOvuE1ptBP9vlllS_7zM3jWs-hfR1Xje_FL7V8g9DiQ5PYCCxLn4pGtHPnt60Sii12pLvz03X_ZVQVRUFFBCl5gGdDNRSUWwaNG7Zb7HEohAJ5_jrvWuJiukC2qF-ixj-EjGdtuMeH1NBsj9BBoT-VE-0DC2vm1GeHU?purpose=inline)

[shadowboxingapp.com](https://shadowboxingapp.com/how-to-throw-a-cross-boxing/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The cross is a straight punch delivered with your rear hand.

Purpose:

- Generate power.
- Follow up a jab.
- Attack openings.
- Create opportunities for combinations.

Technique:

1. Start in your boxing stance.
2. Rotate your rear hip and shoulder.
3. Pivot your rear foot naturally.
4. Extend your rear hand straight.
5. Keep your lead hand protecting your face.
6. Return to your original guard.

Important: Power comes from coordinated movement through your body, not just your arm.

### Punch 3: Hook

[How to Throw a Proper Lead Hook Punch](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/XPvbxdU2yB9S4e5WX4lA2GZyjL7YxpH53Rn5iqcI94sBhbWKz1UApY7vVBeyj6QCQ9w-MDMQF2UIqPLNXGVlQDhygWeHCbsDhmtb_CL1PJaQ_-7hGTmvACSIsl3F5wj_E5HpCDUMV2IKCUz224MRggFCeeWdaxCyBILK0YvM_vo?purpose=inline)

[hayabusafight.com](https://www.hayabusafight.com/blogs/community/hayabusa-striking-basics-how-to-throw-a-lead-hook-with-gabriel-varga?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The hook is a rotational punch delivered in a curved path.

Purpose:

- Attack from an angle.
- Target the head or body.
- Follow a straight punch.
- Exploit openings at close or medium range.

Technique:

1. Begin in your boxing stance.
2. Rotate your hips and torso.
3. Keep your elbow appropriately bent.
4. Turn your body and pivot your foot as needed.
5. Keep your wrist aligned with your forearm.
6. Return immediately to guard.

Avoid swinging your arm widely or twisting your wrist.

### Punch 4: Uppercut

[How To Throw The Uppercut In Boxing in 2025 | Boxing techniques, Boxing workout routine, Martial arts training workouts](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/_uKBbKp5VriZuM7LF34U03RVIjXR-Xl4TlKk1yS9gnqmAXzwdQy_mOG7gljiQzsC47T9mAq_dtYzpsTiWUxqWvvXRS-YVXWtqIi3iRZjlo0rx1F1hQxeMNoKy7zx5pACvSsp6mBmNIvMQBHpMMtoMa95ODOdkLpdsdQbPVPZ9Wc?purpose=inline)

[ph.pinterest.com](https://ph.pinterest.com/pin/how-to-throw-the-uppercut-in-boxing-in-2024--797981627760573372/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The uppercut travels upward toward the target.

Purpose:

- Attack openings at close range.
- Work inside combinations.
- Target the head or body.

Technique:

1. Maintain your boxing stance.
2. Lower your level slightly using your legs.
3. Rotate your hips and torso.
4. Drive the punch upward along a controlled path.
5. Keep the opposite hand protecting your face.
6. Return to guard.

Do not excessively squat or swing your arm upward independently.
