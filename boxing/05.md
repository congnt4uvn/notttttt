## DAY 4 — Mastering Boxing Defense

Today's topic: Blocking, Parrying, Slipping, Rolling, Pull-back Defense & Defensive Footwork.

A professional boxer must develop two essential abilities: landing effective punches and avoiding unnecessary damage.

Today, you'll learn how to defend against attacks while maintaining balance and preparing to counterpunch.

## 1. Learning objectives

By the end of Day 4, you should be able to:

1. Maintain a correct defensive guard.
2. Block straight punches and hooks.
3. Parry a jab using small movements.
4. Slip left and right while maintaining balance.
5. Roll underneath a simulated hook.
6. Understand pull-back defense.
7. Combine defensive movements with footwork.
8. Perform simple defense-to-counter transitions.

Today's drills should use slow, predictable cues from your coach. No hard sparring or intentional head contact.

## 3. Theory — Six essential defensive techniques

### Technique 1 — High guard

[Haunted Legends: The Most Fearless Fighters in Combat Sports • Hayabusa](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/-gUJh71Qp3N7SCMCYyVNxlPA8WaXAM5SC9XTaqrGxdRfKGkjLH-CWpg3dHAV6LIuGfiWqiL2Zop7OsRCu0NXMbvLI37AE9HDT3_kq45cFRKBMyNwb64KNd1eJi8-mQrTc_MDuhpDNX9L9Sgwlr6k8Ozr-qaJGFzOXzqBeFAIjlk?purpose=inline)

[hayabusafight.com](https://www.hayabusafight.com/blogs/community/haunted-legends-the-most-fearless-fighters-in-combat-sports?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The high guard is a fundamental defensive position used to protect the head.

How to perform it:

1. Stand in your boxing stance.
2. Keep your hands near your cheeks and temples.
3. Tuck your chin slightly.
4. Keep your elbows relatively close to your body.
5. Look forward.
6. Maintain balanced foot positioning.

Common mistakes: Closing your eyes, lowering your hands, leaning backward excessively, or keeping your shoulders unnecessarily tense.

A high guard reduces exposure but does not guarantee protection against punches, particularly hooks and body shots.

### Technique 2 — Blocking

[Best Tutorial For Boxing Techniques | Block a Lead Hook](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/9uOoOYTTATascOgXahuUqaJAEnoLf3IsUUbPKn_iSKZ_fA92C6KVZdOajnES_gxN_VlFFDQHBpE2Lw-Y9r1KjOqNLGQ7Xz7K2N9wWn-HV6S_NuOhKn-gzrHng1cKcdjAnOzcY6jA95ODIgEEaJ7h8iYVw36wFaR0Oas4x75ccKU?purpose=inline)

[hayabusafight.ca](https://www.hayabusafight.ca/blogs/community/hayabusa-striking-basics-blocking-a-hook-with-gabriel-varga?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Blocking uses your gloves and arms to intercept incoming punches.

| Incoming punch | Basic defensive response                                           |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Jab            | Catch or block with rear glove                                     |
| Cross          | Catch or block with lead glove                                     |
| Lead hook      | Tighten the guard on the threatened side                           |
| Body hook      | Bring the elbow toward the body without dropping the opposite hand |

Practice the positions without impact first. Your coach can demonstrate the correct positioning.

Key principle: Keep movements compact. Large defensive movements create openings.

### Technique 3 — Parrying

[Parry Punches - Đỉnh cao của kỹ thuật né đòn trong Boxing](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/SblLy1sCnGgXQxb1H2iAnTXAPrJtsNoPfjrCIqfIxXZ71w4bxxgiD33EIDvNI0cUTUS5WbE7-5N8EoFx0kLJC3kpFKXqbua4yydiJACfeyx2uX9fidrVlN3O-LgWi12vMElJIP0Jg1-Q7-ATbIk9isyPxQTeXh0ByFPD-Ynu5Tg?purpose=inline)

[kickfit-sports.com](https://kickfit-sports.com/parry-punches-dinh-cao-cua-ky-thuat-ne-don-trong-boxing/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

A parry redirects a punch away from its intended path.

How to parry a jab:

1. Begin in your guard.
2. Watch your coach's lead hand.
3. As the slow jab approaches, make a small redirection with your rear glove.
4. Keep your opposite hand protecting your face.
5. Return immediately to your guard.

Avoid reaching forward or swinging your hand across your body.

### Technique 4 — Slipping

[How to Defend the Jab in Boxing | H&G Boxing](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/bjYjdUIMEOl-YMcvKyaUxnc8LnV0MPbKN5jF1CRGhBNRfHR4hwvw7tPL6WR0Y7YaMcN-o1rs6RrUynkUQGOrjq0AV6IsYXcsuMx4rnYVEt2s_d7siKPO2ysGCh2V5t3drmLN7ZschDf8W8SCYIr3FAIAzhQGiqaFpWo7d67xCrU?purpose=inline)

[honourandglory.co.uk](https://www.honourandglory.co.uk/blog/how-to-defend-the-jab?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Slipping moves your head slightly off the line of a straight punch.

How to slip:

1. Begin in your boxing stance.
2. Slightly bend your knees.
3. Rotate your torso a small amount.
4. Move your head just outside the imaginary punch line.
5. Keep your eyes forward and hands up.
6. Return to a balanced position.

A slip is a small controlled movement, not a large sideways bend.

Avoid excessive waist bending, twisting your neck, or lowering your head into an unsafe position.

### Technique 5 — Rolling under hooks

[Boxing Slip Rope Workout at Paula Banks blog](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/woI5Xa4ICH8x7UzXFqbsF5ohYdj81kQy0UuCk2m2g_SCGEzLes2XLcQ4eADCG-OMOH06UoZ9joAR4e6zpF7JwYloCKjCk7WOTd_1D8EfoWoqrqCm3osxrgsVhRNN2YAMEZ1xPlQIj3AdrrT2wLxHsbV4W8GxmILE6YjltqSj_8Y?purpose=inline)

[storage.googleapis.com](https://storage.googleapis.com/deraeylifwluee/boxing-slip-rope-workout.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

A roll, sometimes called a weave, is used to move beneath the path of a hook.

How to roll:

1. Start with your guard up.
2. Bend your knees slightly.
3. Move your head through a small U-shaped path.
4. Rotate your torso as you change sides.
5. Rise into a balanced stance.

Your legs and hips should help generate the movement. Do not rely on bending deeply at the waist.

For today's practice, use an imaginary punch or stationary rope, not a real punch aimed at your head.

### Technique 6 — Pull-back defense

[6 Effective Boxing Drills (Using DIY Home Boxing Equipment)](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/a1aR2Ujv-7E5vIMU6LsHvjh7WRh6H2OaUT2aDm1dG88oq421gOE8w6UaAfYN8t-iWyQDtVBdVCpDVrhK7th03SsWFC07ZRbcRKhhoGI5A0eYrpq8i0YBwsxlFlK0Gi9A4bd64haAq_NtXe2Ut1-82K1waA--sBFQHhYylyDViEo?purpose=inline)

[blog.joinfightcamp.com](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/6-at-home-boxing-drills-to-train-using-diy-boxing-equipment/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Pull-back defense creates a small amount of additional distance from an incoming punch.

How to practice:

1. Begin in your stance.
2. Move your upper body slightly backward while staying balanced.
3. Keep your chin tucked and hands up.
4. Return to your stance or take a controlled step away.

Important: Pulling back too far can leave you off balance or vulnerable to a follow-up punch. At your current level, prioritize footwork-based exits over dramatic backward leaning.

### Basic defensive combinations

| Sequence         | Purpose                   |
| ---------------- | ------------------------- |
| Parry → Jab      | Redirect and respond      |
| Slip → Jab       | Move off-line and counter |
| Block → Cross    | Recover guard and counter |
| Roll → Lead Hook | Change level and counter  |
| Step Back → Jab  | Manage distance           |

Practice these against imaginary attacks or slow coach-held targets. Do not counterpunch a partner's head.

Coaching principle: Defend first, regain balance, and only then counter.
