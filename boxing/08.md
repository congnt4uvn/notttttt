Today is your first weekly assessment. You have studied stance, guard, punches, footwork, defense, jab and cross.

The goal today is not to train harder. It is to check your technique, identify weaknesses, and allow your body to recover before moving forward.

Do not do hard sparring or intentional head-contact drills. Ask your coach to supervise the practical assessment.

## 1. Learning objectives

By the end of Day 7, you should be able to:

1. Maintain a balanced boxing stance and proper guard.
2. Move forward, backward and laterally without crossing your feet.
3. Perform a technically controlled jab and cross.
4. Demonstrate basic defensive movements.
5. Combine punches with footwork while maintaining balance.
6. Identify your three biggest technical weaknesses.

Orthodox stance: left foot forward, right hand behind.

Southpaw stance: right foot forward, left hand behind.

Your coach should help determine which stance gives you the best balance, coordination and movement. Do not choose solely based on which hand is dominant.

Shadowboxing combinations:

1. Jab → return to guard.
2. Jab → Cross → return to guard.
3. Jab → Cross → step backward.
4. Jab → Slip → Jab.
5. Jab → Cross → move laterally.

Perform everything slowly and accurately. Do not prioritize punch speed or power.
