   - Feints
   - Angles and pivots
   - Body punching
   - Controlled partner drills

   Days 181–240

   - Defensive transitions
   - Advanced conditioning
   - Coach-supervised sparring if cleared

   Days 241–300

## 4. Theory — Understanding boxing stance (10 minutes)

A boxing stance is the foundation of every punch, defensive movement, and footwork technique.

There are two primary stances.

### A. Orthodox stance

[Heavy Bag Workout for Strength and Cardio - Chicago Athletic Clubs](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/CEwNjjUUp0kMNiRS1Eg7qbwsSCcLspzqh0XTSJN10vyGtcbr_1o_MWg7xqtFntHizET2XdgX6iW4xMdR6uW1jwLC0dD6YrzZIOLV34oIlMYZ7iayP2gC_ipUnVoB0eoEO7R7lhS051aoHl-0_jHiLiC1GbMIUwt_wPb7N29qA0c?purpose=inline)

Orthodox

- Left foot forward.
- Right foot behind.
- Left hand is the lead hand.
- Right hand is the rear hand.

Commonly used by right-handed boxers.

### B. Southpaw stance

[How To Punch Like A Pro — Boss Hunting](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/yy4bjiego49Nn8ejEyCJ4pWHbi3ZifbHIojWOituWXYrlcN41RDdGCbK9fAz2lG8hIrhy-cvzQT8sM09iDNmsAhNLh40YbuSZjLS4MW1KVAr_aB9DlVmZQdbBuqlIMWBrBoDcyXQWmcOXqPTUEvmTm7_U03Dvxsl4_9wHYLnDt0?purpose=inline)

Southpaw

- Right foot forward.
- Left foot behind.
- Right hand is the lead hand.
- Left hand is the rear hand.

Commonly used by left-handed boxers.

### How to determine your stance

Do not choose your stance based only on your dominant hand.

Your coach should evaluate:

- Natural balance.
- Coordination on both sides.
- Lead-hand control.
- Rear-hand power mechanics.
- Footwork comfort.
- Ability to defend from each position.

For today, test both stances with your coach. Do not force yourself into one.

### C. Correct boxing stance

[Maîtriser les techniques de boxe : posture, coups de poing, jeu de jambes et défense  – Fighters Corner](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/Vpise6xwQbMPdtvj2QZ88NbFoXmQ-lPiG4CH2seiwQxt7LR48IN8DuuEZ5SVD_XSTE3LGMgeanavCqmjIN1RfQ3G-Ee0OBYYkLW34Zby09eK1a7JU_VRDBkSixKdsrqb47ntGakCbKJ3HdNE0BszMoUl4M8zeUhmuyqBAh11H_s?purpose=inline)

[fighters-corner.com](https://fighters-corner.com/fr/blogs/fighters-guide/guide-to-boxing-techniques?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Remember these six principles:

1. Feet approximately shoulder-width apart, with a comfortable front-to-back separation.
2. Knees slightly bent.
3. Weight balanced between both feet.
4. Rear heel naturally raised or light, depending on your movement.
5. Hands near your cheeks.
6. Chin slightly tucked, eyes looking forward.

Avoid:

- Standing too upright.
- Feet too close together.
- Feet crossing.
- Leaning too far forward.
- Dropping your hands.
- Locking your knees.

### D. Boxing guard

[Proper Hand Placement | Gabriel Varga | Hayabusa Striking Basics • Hayabusa](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/Ark9o56O1WJsUU1aw_ZOElD3fgCtiMWwHG_6jgDUX_iL-SNoEJXNi7alwYQx6VcGtNyt7_um53IY9iEA3FQPJpTX2VUSIqT_cWuOY1A5lbKe295GcrKiN30zCYFMbGXjnyBIDItiteF9XZtx680yp9HbFJ7-DaaBzB53vJGmLn8?purpose=inline)

[hayabusafight.com](https://www.hayabusafight.com/blogs/community/proper-hand-placement?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Your guard protects your head and helps you prepare to punch.

- Lead hand protects the front and side of your face.
- Rear hand stays close to your cheek.
- Elbows stay relatively close to your body.
- Chin stays tucked.
- Shoulders remain relaxed.

Do not squeeze your shoulders continuously. Excessive tension wastes energy.

### Exercise 3 — Basic footwork (10 minutes)

[Lateral Movement Drills to Improve Your Training | Gloveworx](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/PB2e0Yq3FibOKf7MtkgFlYoNRkkQgrgT54PsLqWq_cGVqd_PxPQUzIRbX74RLx5SrhPUzHJ6wGrjCIZKOObboT4bHLjLZLJ4jI95izCIrXEAmmnyhV2dakwtz2p3n23kc-gCfzI-lEue30TUVcgLWd78bnAJnXaTyQ-0E_eeur8?purpose=inline)

[gloveworx.com](https://www.gloveworx.com/blog/lateral-movement-drills-improve-your-training/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Learn four directions:

| Movement | Technique              |
| -------- | ---------------------- |
| Forward  | Lead foot moves first  |
| Backward | Rear foot moves first  |
| Left     | Left foot moves first  |
| Right    | Right foot moves first |

The other foot follows to restore your stance.

Important: Never cross your feet when moving during normal boxing footwork.

Practice:

- Forward and backward: 3 minutes.
- Left and right: 3 minutes.
- Mixed directions: 4 minutes.

### Exercise 4 — Stance recovery (5 minutes)

Practice:

1. Move one step forward.
2. Move one step backward.
3. Move left.
4. Move right.
5. Return to your original stance.

Your feet should remain balanced and ready to move.
