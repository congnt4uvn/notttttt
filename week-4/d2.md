DAY 23 — Present Simple vs Present Continuous
Đây là phần rất quan trọng.

Present Simple
Thói quen:

I work every day.

Present Continuous
Đang xảy ra:

I am working now.

So sánh:

I watch TV every night.

I am watching TV now.
