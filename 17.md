## DAY 547 — Ambiguity

Câu:

> "The company needs experienced employees."

Hỏi:

> Experienced in what?

> How much experience?

> Why?

> Is experience more important than ability?

## DAY 548 — Clarification

Học:

> What exactly do you mean by...?

> When you say X, are you referring to...?

> Could you clarify what you mean?

> Are you suggesting that...?

### Practice

---

## DAY 549 — Reframing

Ví dụ:

> The problem is that employees don't work hard enough.

Reframe:

> One possible interpretation is that the current work environment may not provide sufficient incentives or support for employees to perform at their best.

---

## DAY 550 — Challenging a Premise

Statement:

> Young people don't read books anymore.

Không phản đối ngay.

Hỏi:

> What do we mean by "read"?

> Are digital books included?

> What evidence supports this claim?

> Is the trend consistent across countries?

---

## DAY 551 — Nuanced Opinion

Chủ đề:

> Is social media harmful?

Không trả lời:

> Yes, it is harmful.

Mà:

> The effects of social media are difficult to characterize in purely positive or negative terms...

Phát triển:

**20–25 phút.**

## 🔥 Một thay đổi rất quan trọng

Từ **Day 531**, hãy bắt đầu luyện:

> **"Say less, mean more."**

Ví dụ:

### B2

> I think this is a very big problem because many people don't understand how technology affects their lives.

### C1

> Technology can have significant consequences for individuals who lack the skills to evaluate its impact critically.

### C2 hướng tới

> **The issue is less the technology itself than the capacity of individuals and institutions to exercise informed judgment over how it is deployed.**
